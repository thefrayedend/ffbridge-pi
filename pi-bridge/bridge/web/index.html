Printers ?

Before you start: turn on LAN-only mode on the printer
  1. On the printer's touch screen open Settings → Network (Wi-Fi or Ethernet).
  2. Turn on LAN-only mode. The printer now shows an 8-character pairing code.
  3. Keep that screen open, then press Scan network here.

Supported: Creator 5 / 5 Pro, AD5X, Adventurer 5M / 5M Pro. The printer and this board must be on the same home network.

Connected printers

AI failure detection ?

While a print runs, the board looks at the printer camera every few seconds with an open-source AI model (from Obico) that recognises "spaghetti" and other failures. Nothing leaves your network.

When it sees a likely failure
Sensitivity
"High" catches problems sooner but may raise more false alarms.

Alerts appear on the printer screen, as a pop-up in Mainsail, and in Discord if set up under General.

Send prints from OrcaSlicer ?

  1. In OrcaSlicer, click the Wi-Fi icon next to your printer (Connection settings).
  2. Host type: Octo/Klipper
  3. Hostname, IP or URL: this-board:7125
    If your router gives the board a new address one day, ffbridge.local:7125 keeps working too.
  4. Leave the API key empty and press Test. Use Print to send and start a job.

Mainsail

Each printer also has a full Mainsail page (the Klipper web interface). It runs separately from this screen; OrcaSlicer's Device tab shows it. Many Mainsail panels have no effect on FlashForge printers.

Printer network ?

Off

Optional. The board's Wi-Fi becomes a private network just for your printers, and the board joins your home network with a network cable. The printers can't reach the internet, and nothing on the internet can reach them. You still use everything through this page, Mainsail and OrcaSlicer as before.

Printers on this network

After you connect a printer to the printer Wi-Fi, the bridge finds it at its new address by itself within about 2 minutes.

Internet for the printers

Blocked unless you allow it, for example to download a printer firmware update. It switches itself off again.

Switch on

  1. Plug a network cable from your router into the board. Check that this page still opens over the cable (Wi-Fi tab: Ethernet connected).
  2. Choose a name and password for the printer Wi-Fi below and press Switch on. The board's own Wi-Fi connection to your home network stops.
  3. On each printer's screen, connect its Wi-Fi to the new network.
Good to know before you switch on
  • FlashPrint, the FlashForge app or OrcaSlicer's own FlashForge connection can't reach the printers directly any more. Use this page, Mainsail or OrcaSlicer's Klipper/Octo connection (port 7125…), which go through the board.
  • Printer firmware updates need internet: allow it for an hour from this page when you want to update.
  • The board's small Wi-Fi antenna has less range than a router. Keep the printers reasonably close to the board.
  • The board gives the printers the correct time, so their clocks stay right without internet.
  • To undo it, press Switch off and connect the printers to your home Wi-Fi again.

Switch off

The board goes back to your home Wi-Fi (if one is saved). Connect each printer to your home Wi-Fi again on its screen.

Updates ?

Backup & restore ?

Saves your printers (with pairing codes), notification, AI and page settings, print history and Mainsail settings in one file. Print files are not included.

Download backup

The board also keeps an up-to-date copy as ffbridge-backup.json on the SD card's FFBRIDGE drive. Before flashing a new card, copy that file off — put it on the new card's FFBRIDGE drive and your settings come back by themselves.
