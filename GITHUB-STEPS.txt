HOW TO GET THE FINISHED SD CARD IMAGE (one time, about 15 minutes of clicking)
==============================================================================

Why GitHub? Building the image takes a Linux computer with an ARM processor.
GitHub lends you one for free. You upload the files, press a button, and it
builds the image for you.

You need: the Orange Pi Zero 4 image you downloaded, and the Chrome or Edge browser.
(Don't upload this file, GITHUB-STEPS.txt. It's only the instructions.)


STEP 1 - Make a free GitHub account (skip this if you already have one)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
Go to https://github.com and click "Sign up". Follow the steps.


STEP 2 - Make a new, empty project ("repository")
-------------------------------------------------
1. Go to https://github.com/new
2. Repository name:  ffbridge-pi
3. Choose "Public". (The free build computer is only free for public projects.)
4. Do NOT tick "Add a README file". Leave everything else as it is.
5. Click the green "Create repository" button.


STEP 3 - Upload the files
-------------------------
1. On the page you see now, click the blue link "uploading an existing file".
2. In File Explorer, open the folder  FFBridge-for-GitHub
   (it's in your FlashForgeUI-Electron-Creator5-main - BTT pi folder).
3. Press Ctrl+A to select everything in it, then drag it all into the browser
   window, onto the box that says "Drag files here".
4. Wait until the list of files stops growing (about 60 files).
5. Scroll down and click the green "Commit changes" button.


STEP 4 - Add the "build" button (copy and paste one file)
---------------------------------------------------------
1. On your project's main page click "Add file", then "Create new file".
2. In the file name box at the top, type exactly:
       .github/workflows/build-image.yml
   (When you type each "/", GitHub turns the part before it into a folder.
   That's normal.)
3. On your PC, open  FFBridge-for-GitHub\pi-bridge\github-workflows\build-image.yml
   with Notepad (right-click > Open with > Notepad).
   Press Ctrl+A, then Ctrl+C.
4. Click in the big empty box in the browser and press Ctrl+V.
5. Click the green "Commit changes..." button, then "Commit changes" again.


STEP 5 - Upload the Orange Pi image you downloaded
--------------------------------------------------
1. Go back to your project's main page (click "ffbridge-pi" at the top).
2. On the right side, under "Releases", click "Create a new release".
3. Click "Choose a tag" (or "Select tag"), type:   base-image
   then click "+ Create new tag: base-image on publish".
4. Release title:  Orange Pi base image
5. Drag the Orange Pi Zero 4 image file you downloaded onto the box
   "Attach binaries by dropping them here".
   This can take a few minutes. Wait until it finishes.
   (It must be the Debian or Ubuntu "server" image for the Orange Pi Zero 4.
   GitHub accepts files up to 2 GB.)
6. Click the green "Publish release" button.


STEP 6 - Press the build button
-------------------------------
1. Click the "Actions" tab at the top of your project.
   If you see a green button "I understand my workflows, go ahead and enable them",
   click it.
2. On the left, click "Build SD card image".
3. On the right, click "Run workflow", then the green "Run workflow" button.
4. Refresh the page after a few seconds. A yellow dot means it's building.
   It takes about 30 to 60 minutes. You can close the browser in the meantime.
   - Green tick = done.
   - Red X = something went wrong. Click on it, take a screenshot and show it to Claude.


STEP 7 - Download the finished image
------------------------------------
1. On your project's main page, on the right, click "Releases".
2. Open "Latest SD card image".
3. Click  ffbridge-orangepi-zero4.img.xz  to download it.
4. Write it to the SD card with balenaEtcher (https://etcher.balena.io)
   or Raspberry Pi Imager ("Choose OS" > "Use custom").
   You don't need to unzip it first.

That's the file you give to other people too. They only need step 7.
